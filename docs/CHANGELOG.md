# v71 Search Robustness, Specialty Organization, Remaining Coverage Gaps

- **Fixed a real search bug** found through systematic query testing: the active search engine hard-rejects a resource unless it matches *every* detected intent (crisis/therapy/IOP-PHP/SUD/testing/ADHD/autism/youth/virtual/county, now also eating disorder/perinatal/LGBTQ+/older-adult/veteran/DV-SA/homelessness). Compound queries like "eating disorder testing" or "crisis eating disorder" returned zero results even though relevant resources exist, because no single resource satisfied every detected concept. Added a fallback pass: when strict AND-of-intents finds nothing, it relaxes to "at least one detected intent matches" (still bound by free-token/location/payer/status), so a reasonable compound query never dead-ends. (An earlier version of this fix matched *everything* when free tokens were fully consumed by intent recognition -- caught and corrected during testing before it shipped.)
- **Added a second "Specialty" filter facet** (`specialtyTiles`/`specialtyTags`, separate from the existing 8 program-type categories) so cross-cutting needs -- Eating disorder, ADHD/neuropsych testing, Autism/developmental, Perinatal/postpartum, LGBTQ+ affirming, Older adult, Veteran, Domestic violence/sexual assault, Homelessness/housing -- are filterable on their own instead of only discoverable by knowing the right search term. Backfilled specialty tags onto the 15 previously-added eating-disorder/ADHD/autism resources, and wired the specialty system into the search engine's intent/evidence model so it doubles as a search accuracy improvement.
- **Closed the remaining 6 coverage gaps** the app's own `V57_GAPS` list had been flagging, with 15 new verified resources (parallel research passes, each independently fetched/corroborated): Postpartum Support International HelpLine, RUHS Maternal Mental Health/MOMS Program, and an individual perinatal psychiatrist for perinatal care; The Trevor Project and the Inland Empire LGBTQ+ Center (TruEvolution) for LGBTQ+ affirming care -- including an honest caveat that 988's LGBTQ+ "press 3" routing was discontinued in July 2025 and its relaunch status is unconfirmed; San Bernardino's Age Wise Program, RUHS Older Adult Services, and the Alzheimer's Association SoCal helpline for older adults; the Veterans Crisis Line, VA Loma Linda, and both counties' Veteran Service Offices for veterans; the National DV Hotline and Alternatives to Domestic Violence (Riverside) for domestic violence/sexual assault; and Riverside County's HomeConnect/Coordinated Entry System for homelessness/housing. `V57_GAPS` is now empty.
